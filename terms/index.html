Project terms.

The practical foundations of an Anvaya engagement.

Scope and timelines

You send the floorplan (a PDF, CAD file or clear phone photo) and the finishes you plan to use. We confirm the rooms, views and delivery date in writing before work starts. Stills take 5 working days, a 360° tour 7 and Premium 10. Express delivery in about half the time costs 25% more.

Two rounds of changes are included, each sent as one consolidated list. They cover furniture, finishes, lighting and camera angles. Moving walls or resizing rooms after you approve the layout is quoted as a new floorplan. Further rounds are ₹1,999 each.

Pricing and payment

Packages are priced per unit type, meaning one floorplan such as a 2BHK. Regular prices are Stills ₹14,999, 360° Tour ₹39,999 for a 2BHK or ₹54,999 for a 3BHK, and Premium from ₹89,999. Our first 3 builders get offer prices (₹9,999, ₹24,999, ₹34,999 and ₹59,999) in return for a testimonial and permission to show the work. Each further unit type in the same project is 35% off. Websites, enquiry follow-up and video ads are quoted per project. Every builder pays from the same published price list.

We take a 50% advance to start and the balance before final files, by UPI or bank transfer. Previews carry a watermark until payment is complete. Third-party charges, such as WhatsApp message fees, are listed and agreed up front. No GST is added at present.

Confidentiality

Your floorplans and renders are never shown to anyone, including other builders, without your written permission. We sign an NDA on request at no extra cost.

Ownership and handover

Once the final payment is made, you own the delivered images, videos and tour, and may use them in any marketing. Anvaya keeps its own tools, scripts and furniture library, which are not part of the handover.

Renders in advertising

Renders show the approved plan as an artist’s impression; finishes and furniture are indicative. Under RERA, brochures and ads must carry your project’s RERA registration number, and renders should be labelled as artist’s impressions. Keeping ads accurate to the registered project is the promoter’s responsibility.

Defect warranty

For 14 days after delivery, report any broken link, rendering error or room that does not match the approved plan, and we fix it at no cost. Tour hosting is included for 12 months, then ₹4,999 a year.
