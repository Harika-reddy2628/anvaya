Payment
50% advance to start, 50% before final files. UPI or bank transfer. Previews carry a watermark until paid.
Review rounds
Two rounds of changes are included, sent as one consolidated list each. Further rounds are ₹1,999 each.
Scope changes
Changes cover furniture, finishes, lighting and camera angles. Moving walls or resizing rooms after you approve the layout is quoted as a new floorplan.
Confidentiality
Your plans and renders are never shown to anyone without your written permission. We sign an NDA on request at no cost.
Same price for all
Every builder pays from this same published list. The founding offer is open to the first 3 builders who sign, whoever they are.
Ownership
You own the final images, videos and tour once the final payment is made. Our tools and furniture library stay with Anvaya.
RERA
Renders are artist’s impressions of the approved plan. Label them so on brochures and ads, next to your project’s RERA registration number.
Warranty
14 days to report a broken link, rendering error or wrong room. We fix it at no cost.
Third-party costs
WhatsApp message charges and paid hosting, if ever needed, are listed and agreed up front. No GST is added at present.

Prices are per unit type (one floorplan, such as a 2BHK). Exact deliverables and dates are confirmed in writing before work starts. Full details in the project terms.
