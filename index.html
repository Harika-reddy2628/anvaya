05/QUESTIONS

Before you start.

01Which practices can I start with today?

Digital Presence and Spatial Visuals are available for new projects. Digital Products and Intelligent Operations are coming soon.

02Do I need a complete brief?

No. Tell us what you are building, improving or trying to make clearer. You do not need to have every answer yet — a useful starting point is enough.

03Can one project cross more than one practice?

Yes. Choose one service, or bring us a problem that crosses all four. We shape the right combination around what needs to move.

04How many review rounds are included?

Two rounds of changes per stage. Further rounds are quoted before work begins, so nothing is billed by surprise. See pricing.

05How do payments work?

50% to begin and 50% on final verified delivery, or an agreed, fully funded platform escrow. The full details are in the project terms.

06What happens after I send an enquiry?

We read the context, find the right starting point and come back with a direction — a useful response, not a generic sales script.

07Where is the studio based?

Anvaya is a technology and design studio in India, working globally.
